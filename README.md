## My rules

- **Free only, never paid** — I only take free mints and claims; a paid mint is never worth it for me.
- **Verify before you trust** — check the contract on-chain (code, Sourcify, owner) before any mint.
- **Never share your phrase or private key** — no tool here will ever ask for one. Every repo above is read-only or docs-only.
- **Share what you learn** — that's why these repos exist.
